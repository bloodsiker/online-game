# Промпты карт ресурсных локаций

Референс стиля — реальный скриншот игровой карты сверху (тёмная/вулканическая зона): вид строго сверху, рельеф передан мягкой светотенью (не плоско), кусты/камни/дым нарисованы как отдельные текстурные пятна поверх фоновой земли, а не тайлятся геометрически, вьющаяся тропа с неровными краями.

## Нейтральный рельеф (кусты, тропинки, камни, руины построек)

```
A top-down (bird's-eye, near-vertical camera) painted game map terrain tile,
seamless-tileable outdoor ground texture in a dark fantasy MMORPG style.

Ground: uneven natural relief — subtle rises and hollows suggested through
soft directional shading (not flat), a mottled dirt-and-stone surface texture
in muted greyish-brown tones, with patches of sparse dry grass breaking
through here and there.

Features scattered across the terrain (painted as distinct textured clusters,
not tiled repeats):
- A winding worn dirt/stone path curving diagonally across the frame, edges
  irregular and worn rather than a clean ribbon, slightly lighter in tone
  than the surrounding ground
- Several dark, dense bush/shrub clusters of varying sizes, rendered as soft
  rounded masses of shadowed foliage, scattered off to the sides of the path
- A handful of loose boulders and rock clusters grouped in small piles at a
  few points, catching a soft highlight on their upper faces
- One or two small ruined or half-built stone structures at the edges of the
  frame (a crumbling low wall, an old stone pillar, or a small watchtower
  base) — implying the world continues beyond the frame, not full buildings

Style: painterly top-down game-map art, muted natural palette (greys, dull
browns, muted greens), soft ambient occlusion around clusters to seat them
into the ground, no hard tile seams, no character or creature sprites, no UI
elements, no text. Lighting reads as overcast daylight from directly above.
```

## Светлая лесная локация (деревья, 2000×2000)

```
A top-down (bird's-eye, near-vertical camera) painted game map terrain tile,
seamless-tileable outdoor ground texture in a bright fantasy MMORPG forest
style. Image size 2000x2000 pixels, square 1:1 aspect ratio.

Ground: gently uneven forest floor with soft relief shading (not flat) —
warm dappled sunlight filtering through unseen canopy above, mottled
earthy-green and light-brown ground texture, patches of grass and fallen
leaves scattered naturally.

Features scattered across the terrain (painted as distinct textured clusters,
not tiled repeats):
- A winding worn dirt path curving diagonally across the frame, edges soft
  and irregular, sun-bleached tan color, slightly lighter than the
  surrounding ground
- Several full tree canopies viewed from directly above — rounded, layered
  clusters of bright green leaves with visible texture and a few warm
  highlight patches where sunlight hits the top of the foliage, scattered
  unevenly with small gaps of open ground between them, a few tree trunks
  just barely peeking out at canopy edges
- A scattering of smaller bushes and undergrowth clusters in a slightly
  darker green, tucked between the trees
- A handful of pale grey boulders and loose rock clusters grouped near the
  path, catching a soft warm highlight on their upper faces
- One or two small stone structures at the edges of the frame (an old
  moss-covered well, a crumbling low wall, or a small shrine) — implying the
  world continues beyond the frame, not full buildings

Style: painterly top-down game-map art, warm bright daylight palette (fresh
greens, warm tan path, soft golden highlights), gentle ambient occlusion
around tree canopies and rocks to seat them into the ground, no hard tile
seams, no character or creature sprites, no UI elements, no text. Lighting
reads as clear midday sun from directly above.

--ar 1:1 --size 2000x2000
```
