# Промпт для картинки: Печать Резонанса

Единый house-style инвентарных иконок (как у осколков/артефактов в этой же
механике точки, см. `shard-prompts.md`, `epic125-shard-and-artifact-prompts.md`):
плоская насыщенная 2D-иконка, белое свечение по контуру, прозрачный фон.
Предмет появится рядом с осколками/артефактами в той же кузне — держим
визуальную консистентность, а не стиль «Купеческая Печать» (тот был
живописный/с синим фоном — для другой категории предметов).

```text
Stylized 2D fantasy MMORPG inventory icon, "Seal of Resonance," an ornate metal seal-stamp. A short bronze and silver stamp tool with a carved wooden-and-metal handle, its round face carved with a glowing cyan-white rune of concentric resonance rings, faint visible ripples of energy radiating outward from the face like sound waves frozen in the air, bold illustrated shapes. Prioritize a striking loot-icon silhouette over physically accurate metalwork.

Hand-painted 2D game asset in the style of classic browser fantasy MMORPG inventory icons. Bold illustrated contours, exaggerated proportions, saturated colors, broad painted light-and-shadow shapes, and a few sharp graphic highlights. Suggest volume through illustration, not realistic materials or physically accurate lighting. Simplified ornamentation, no tiny engraving, no realistic material refraction, no surface microtexture. Serious heroic fantasy, not cute or chibi. No photography, jewelry advertising, photorealism, 3D rendering, ray tracing, depth of field, or cinematic scene. Nearly frontal view, centered upright composition, instantly readable at 60×60 pixels. The seal itself fills approximately 85-90% of the canvas along its longest dimension, with the entire item visible without cropping.

IMPORTANT: the stamp MUST be surrounded by a thick, unmistakable bright white glowing outline — a solid bright white rim light tracing the entire outer silhouette, then softly fading outward into a white halo. This white glow is a mandatory, highly visible design element, not a subtle effect. Preserve the seal's own colors inside the outline; only the glow around the edge is white. Fully transparent background with a true alpha channel (everything outside the glow is pure transparent alpha, not white) — no colored background, no checkerboard pattern, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no watermark, and no external frame. PNG format, 1024×1024 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
