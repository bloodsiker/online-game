# Промпты для картинок артефактов (rare, египетский набор)

Намеренно НЕ копирую композицию common/uncommon (тёмные крылья + трофей +
цепь) — нужна новая узнаваемая форма для этого тира. Взял аутентичный
египетский мотив: золотой овальный картуш (рамка-медальон) с гравировкой
в духе иероглифов вместо крыльев, внутри — сам реликт. Стиль описания и
усиленное белое свечение — тот же house-style, что и у остальных предметов.

Общий шаблон:

```text
Stylized 2D fantasy MMORPG inventory icon, "[NAME]," an Egyptian relic amulet. A bold golden oval cartouche frame with angular hieroglyph-inspired engravings along its border encircles the central relic, no wings, no demon motifs. A small glowing [GEM_COLOR] gemstone sits at the top center of the frame, flanked by two small stylized sun-disk engravings. Inside the cartouche, [CENTRAL_RELIC], rendered in bold readable [RELIC_MATERIAL] shapes. A dark metal chain loop with two or three visible links is attached at the very top. Prioritize a striking loot-icon silhouette over believable jewelry construction.

Hand-painted 2D game asset in the style of classic browser fantasy MMORPG inventory icons. Bold illustrated contours, exaggerated proportions, saturated colors, broad painted light-and-shadow shapes, and a few sharp graphic highlights. Suggest volume through illustration, not realistic materials or physically accurate lighting. Simplified ornamentation, no tiny engraving, no realistic material refraction, no surface microtexture. Serious heroic fantasy, not cute or chibi. No photography, jewelry advertising, photorealism, 3D rendering, ray tracing, depth of field, or cinematic scene. Nearly frontal view, centered upright composition, instantly readable at 60×60 pixels. The pendant itself fills approximately 85-90% of the canvas along its longest dimension, with the entire item visible without cropping.

IMPORTANT: the pendant MUST be surrounded by a thick, unmistakable bright white glowing outline — a solid bright white rim light tracing the entire outer silhouette, then softly fading outward into a white halo. This white glow is a mandatory, highly visible design element, not a subtle effect. Preserve the pendant's own colors inside the outline; only the glow around the edge is white. Fully transparent background with a true alpha channel (everything outside the glow is pure transparent alpha, not white) — no colored background, no checkerboard pattern, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no watermark, and no external frame. PNG format, 1024×1024 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
