# Промпты для картинок: гримуары заклинаний (4 типа)

Единый house-style инвентарных иконок (как у осколков/артефактов/печатей,
см. `shard-prompts.md`, `resonance-seal-prompt.md`): плоская насыщенная 2D-иконка,
белое свечение по контуру, прозрачный фон. Все 28 книг заклинаний визуально
делятся на 4 типа по `magic_skills.type` — цвет и символ на обложке говорит
игроку, что внутри, без необходимости читать тултип. Хил визуально относится
к тому же типу, что баффы (обе поддерживающие, золотой тон) — отдельного
5-го типа не вводим.

## 1. Атакующие (attack) — Огненный шар, Молния, Испепеляющий вихрь...

```text
Stylized 2D fantasy MMORPG inventory icon, a closed spellbook grimoire bound in dark scorched-red leather, its cover branded with a glowing orange-red rune shaped like a jagged flame or lightning bolt, metal corner clasps with an ember-orange glow seeping from the edges of the pages, a cracked leather strap wrapped around it, bold illustrated shapes. Prioritize a striking loot-icon silhouette over physically accurate bookbinding.

Hand-painted 2D game asset in the style of classic browser fantasy MMORPG inventory icons. Bold illustrated contours, exaggerated proportions, saturated colors, broad painted light-and-shadow shapes, and a few sharp graphic highlights. Suggest volume through illustration, not realistic materials or physically accurate lighting. Simplified ornamentation, no tiny engraving, no realistic material refraction, no surface microtexture. Serious heroic fantasy, not cute or chibi. No photography, jewelry advertising, photorealism, 3D rendering, ray tracing, depth of field, or cinematic scene. Nearly frontal view, centered upright composition, instantly readable at 60×60 pixels. The book fills approximately 85-90% of the canvas along its longest dimension, with the entire item visible without cropping.

IMPORTANT: the grimoire MUST be surrounded by a thick, unmistakable bright white glowing outline — a solid bright white rim light tracing the entire outer silhouette, then softly fading outward into a white halo. This white glow is a mandatory, highly visible design element, not a subtle effect. Preserve the book's own colors inside the outline; only the glow around the edge is white. Fully transparent background with a true alpha channel (everything outside the glow is pure transparent alpha, not white) — no colored background, no checkerboard pattern, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no watermark, and no external frame. PNG format, 1024×1024 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
