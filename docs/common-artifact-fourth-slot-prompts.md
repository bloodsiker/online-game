# 4-й обычный артефакт пула осколков — варианты (v3, правильный icon-стиль)

Композиция — по реальным картинкам 924-926 (симметричная пара тёмных
крыльев обрамляет трофей, акцентный камень сверху, цепь). Стиль описания —
по `premium-artifact-pendant-prompts.md` (927-930): это установленный
house-style для **предметных иконок** («Stylized 2D fantasy MMORPG
inventory icon», плоские насыщенные формы, жирный контур), а не стиль
бестиария боссов («painterly illustration, dramatic lighting»), который я
ошибочно подмешал в v2.

Общий шаблон:

```text
Stylized 2D fantasy MMORPG inventory icon, “[NAME],” a gothic amulet pendant. A symmetrical pair of sharp dark obsidian-black demon wings curve outward and upward, framing the central relic like heraldic wings, their edges jagged and angular. A small glowing [GEM_COLOR] gemstone sits at the top center, nestled between the base of the wings. Below it, [CENTRAL_RELIC], rendered in bold readable [RELIC_MATERIAL] shapes. A dark metal chain loop with two or three visible links is attached at the very top. Prioritize a striking loot-icon silhouette over believable jewelry construction.

Hand-painted 2D game asset in the style of classic browser fantasy MMORPG inventory icons. Bold illustrated contours, exaggerated proportions, saturated colors, broad painted light-and-shadow shapes, and a few sharp graphic highlights. Suggest volume through illustration, not realistic materials or physically accurate lighting. Simplified ornamentation, no tiny engraving, no realistic material refraction, no surface microtexture. Serious heroic fantasy, not cute or chibi. No photography, jewelry advertising, photorealism, 3D rendering, ray tracing, depth of field, or cinematic scene. Nearly frontal view, centered upright composition, instantly readable at 60×60 pixels. The pendant itself fills approximately 85-90% of the canvas along its longest dimension, with the entire item visible without cropping.

Fully transparent background with a true alpha channel, no colored background, no checkerboard pattern, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no watermark, and no external frame. PNG format, 1024×1024 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
