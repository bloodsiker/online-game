# Промпт для иконки «Сертификат «Смена аватара»» (id=1829)

Стиль — по образцу уже реальных сертификатов (`docs/rename-certificate-icon-prompt.md`,
текущий `/img/resource/sert_obraz.gif` у «Смены расы»): рулон пергамента,
перевязанный лентой, с восковой печатью. Символ на печати меняется под смысл
предмета — здесь это овальное зеркало/портретная рамка (смена облика), а не
вопросительный знак (смена имени) или силуэт фигуры (смена расы).

## Актуальный вариант: крупный предмет с усиленным белым свечением

```text
Add a clearly visible bright white outline along the entire outer silhouette, surrounded by a stronger, moderately wide luminous white outer glow. Use a crisp white rim near the object and a soft radiant halo that gradually fades into full transparency. The glow should remain noticeable when the icon is reduced to 60×60 pixels and help separate the item from dark or textured backgrounds. Keep the parchment and symbols sharp and fully readable; do not overexpose the object or turn the glow into an opaque white backdrop.

Classic hand-painted fantasy game art, detailed textures, strong silhouette, soft dimensional shading, crisp edges. Clearly recognizable at 60×60 pixels. Large, close-up centered composition: the object itself occupies approximately 85–90% of the canvas along its longest dimension, with minimal empty space. Keep the entire object and its glow inside the frame without cropping, leaving only enough transparent margin for the halo to fade out naturally.

No text, letters, numbers, borders, watermarks, scenery, backdrop, or cast shadow. Fully transparent background with a true alpha channel—not white and not a checkerboard pattern. PNG format, square 1:1 aspect ratio, 1024×1024 pixels.
```
